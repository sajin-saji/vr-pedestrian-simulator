# Team and credits

**Development of an Immersive Pedestrian Simulator with Dynamic Virtual Traffic** was a
seven-member collaborative project in the MSc Mechatronics and Cyber-Physical Systems
programme at Deggendorf Institute of Technology.

## Team

- Ashish Uriviti
- Kowshik Karnam Kesavalu
- Krishna Gaadha
- Muhammad Talha
- Naveenraj Palanisamy
- Sai Kiran Vankayalapati
- Sajin Saji

We built the simulator together across environment design, road and traffic
simulation, C# gameplay, NPC pedestrians, audio, VR integration and testing.

The full team report is in [VR_Pedestrian_Simulator_Report.pdf](VR_Pedestrian_Simulator_Report.pdf).

## Assets

- The figures in [`figures/`](figures/) are from the team's Unity project.
- The original project used third-party assets that are not redistributed here,
  including Road Architect, the Multiversal Vehicle Controller, Unity Asset Store
  environment packs, Mixamo characters, Pixabay sounds and the Meta XR SDKs.
- The sounds in [`../Assets/Audio/Demo/`](../Assets/Audio/Demo/) were synthesised by
  [`../tools/generate_audio.py`](../tools/generate_audio.py) for the
  desktop version.
